Getting there

From At Parramatta's Parking and Getting around pages, read 12 Nov 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and timetables.

Train

It is a 30-minute ride from Sydney's Town Hall to Parramatta Station. Harris Park and Westmead stations are close by.

Lifts and step-free access: not stated on the page.

Light rail

Westmead to Carlingford via the Parramatta CBD and Camellia. 12 km of two-way track and 16 stops.

Step-free access: not stated on the page.

Ferry

The F3 Parramatta River ferry runs from Parramatta Quay to Circular Quay.

Boarding access: not stated on the page.

Parking

Up to 18 hours for $14 at the Justice Precinct and Parramatta Station (Wentworth Street) car parks. Fennell Street is $10.70 all day. Both first-named car parks give up to 2 hours free on Sundays.

Accessible bays: not stated on the page. The Parking Finder lists accessible parking.
